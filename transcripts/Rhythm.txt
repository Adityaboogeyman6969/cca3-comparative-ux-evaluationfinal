Hi my name is rhythm tyagi and i am reviewing Aditya Marathe's project ,Although the redesigned Khelmart website aims to improve usability, I think there are still some areas that deserve attention.

First, the product filters need to work consistently. If I select a particular brand or price range, the results should match those selections. There should also be an easy way to clear filters and return to the full product catalogue.

Second, stock availability should be accurate and easy to notice. Customers should not have to proceed through several steps before discovering that a product is unavailable.

Third, product pages should present specifications, ratings, prices, and delivery information in a consistent format. This is especially important when customers are comparing similar products.

I would also pay attention to broken product images and repeated footer links. These issues may appear small, but they can make a website feel less reliable and less carefully maintained.

Another area for improvement is customer support. Users should be able to find information about delivery, returns, and contacting the seller without searching through multiple pages.

My recommendation would be to prioritise functional issues first, followed by improvements to the visual layout. A clean design is valuable, but the website must also work as expected.

Overall, the redesign is a useful step forward, and testing it with real users would help identify which changes provide the greatest benefit.
