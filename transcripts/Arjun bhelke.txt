Hello my name is Arjun bhelke and i will be  reviewing Aditya Marathe's project,The most useful improvements in the redesigned Khelmart website, from my perspective, are the product filters, clearer product information, and better visibility of stock availability.

When I purchase sports equipment, I usually have specific requirements. I might be looking for a particular brand, a certain price range, or a product suitable for my level of experience. A well-organised filtering system can make this process much faster.

I also think that product details play a major role in the buying decision. Customers should be able to understand the important specifications without having to search through multiple sections of a page.

Another useful feature is clearer availability information. It can be frustrating to spend time comparing a product and discover later that it is unavailable. Showing accurate stock information early in the process can help customers make better decisions.

Customer ratings and reviews can also help people understand the experiences of other buyers. However, these should be genuine and displayed consistently.

If I could add one more feature, it would be a sports-specific buying guide. For example, a beginner buying a badminton racket could receive simple guidance about weight, grip, and playing style.

Overall, I think these improvements make the shopping journey more practical because they address the information customers actually need before purchasing sports equipment.
