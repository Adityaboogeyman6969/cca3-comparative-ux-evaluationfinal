Hi my name is arhaan mulani and i am reviewing Aditya Marathe's project When I first explored the redesigned Khelmart website, my main impression was that the shopping experience felt more organised. The original website had several product categories and a lot of information competing for attention, which could make it difficult to decide where to start.

What I liked about the redesigned version was the focus on clearer navigation and product presentation. Since Khelmart sells different types of sports equipment, it is important for users to find the right category without spending too much time searching.

I also liked the idea of presenting product information, ratings, and availability more clearly. When buying sports equipment, I don’t just look at the price. I want to understand what I am purchasing and whether the product is suitable for my needs.

Another positive aspect is the more structured layout. It makes the website easier to scan, especially for someone who already knows what they want to buy.

One thing I would still improve is the homepage hierarchy. The most important categories and search options should be immediately visible, without too many competing promotional elements.

Overall, I think the redesign has the potential to make Khelmart more user-friendly. It focuses on helping customers find products efficiently rather than making them navigate through unnecessary information.
