Hi my name is ishwari kharat and i am reviewing Aditya Marathe's project, Overall, I think the redesigned Khelmart website has the potential to provide a smoother shopping experience than the original.

The biggest strength is its focus on organising the shopping journey. Customers should be able to find a product category, narrow down their options, review product details, and move towards checkout without feeling lost.

I also appreciate the focus on clearer product information and stock visibility. These details help customers make more informed decisions and reduce the possibility of disappointment later.

However, I would not judge the website only by how attractive it looks. A successful shopping experience also depends on whether the search and filters work properly, product images load correctly, and the checkout process is easy to complete.

I would also want to see clear delivery charges, return policies, and genuine customer reviews. These factors influence trust just as much as the visual design.

If I were evaluating the final prototype, I would ask users to complete a few realistic tasks, such as finding a cricket bat within a budget and locating the delivery information for a product.

I would then observe how easily they complete those tasks and where they encounter difficulties.

My final opinion is that the redesign addresses important usability concerns. With functional testing and further user feedback, it could become a more organised and reliable shopping experience for sports customers.
