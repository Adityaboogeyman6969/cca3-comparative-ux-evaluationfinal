hello my name is amey tarmale and i am reviewing aditya Marathe's project,My main priority when shopping on Khelmart is finding the exact sports equipment I need without wasting time. For example, if I want to buy a cricket bat, I would like to narrow down my options by brand, price, and other relevant specifications.

The original website’s product discovery experience could be confusing when there were many products to browse. The redesigned concept addresses this by focusing on more organised categories and useful product filters.

I particularly like the idea of making filters easier to understand. Instead of repeatedly scrolling through products that do not match my requirements, I should be able to shortlist relevant options quickly.

Another important part of usability is knowing where I am in the shopping journey. The transition from a product listing to the product details page, cart, and checkout should feel straightforward.

I would also like the website to clearly indicate when a product is unavailable or when my selected filters return no results. This would prevent unnecessary confusion.

One improvement I would suggest is adding a comparison option for similar products, especially cricket bats, badminton rackets, and other equipment with different specifications.

Overall, the redesign focuses on making product discovery more efficient. For me, a good shopping website is one that helps me complete my task with fewer unnecessary steps.
